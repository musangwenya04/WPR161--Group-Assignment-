Meet the Team

Organizers, Artists, and Vendors

Participants & Contributors

Green Fest is powered by passionate individuals and organizations who believe in a greener tomorrow. Here's who you'll meet:

  • EcoVibe Team - Event coordinators bringing eco awareness to life.
  • Solar Sound Collective - Performers who power their shows entirely via solar energy.
  • Green Bites - Organic and locally sourced food vendors.
  • Urban Gardeners - Teaching composting, urban farming, and permaculture basics.
  • EcoTech Lab - Demos of solar gadgets, recycling tech, and DIY systems.
  • Kids Go Green - Arts, crafts, and games to get young minds excited about nature.

Interested in volunteering or being a vendor? Contact us to join the movement!
