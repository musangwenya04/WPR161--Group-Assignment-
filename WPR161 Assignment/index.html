🌿 Green Fest 2025 🌿

Eco. Fun. Community.

Welcome to Green Fest!

Green Fest is a fictional community event celebrating eco-friendly living. Held every April, it's a fun, family-friendly gathering filled with live music, workshops, games, food stalls, and sustainability-focused vendors.

Join us for a day of fun, learning, and action as we celebrate green living and inspire our community to go sustainable.

  • 🌱 Eco-stalls and markets
  • 🎵 Solar-powered live music
  • 👩‍🏫 Educational green workshops
  • 🎨 Kid's arts & crafts corner
  • 🌺 Plant-your-own tree activity
